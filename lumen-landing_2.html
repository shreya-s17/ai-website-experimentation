Questions

Before you reserve.

Does Lumen need a camera to know who's home?

No. Presence sensing runs on mmWave radar, the same kind of tech used in some health monitors — it detects people, not images.

What happens to my deposit if I change my mind?

It's refunded in full, any time before your unit ships. No form, just a reply to your confirmation email.

Can it control the smart home gear I already own?

Yes. Lumen speaks Thread and Wi-Fi 6 natively, and bridges most major smart home ecosystems at launch.

Does it record conversations?

Lumen listens locally and discards audio right after processing it, unless you explicitly ask it to save a note.

When do founder units actually ship?

March 2027, in the order reservations were placed. We'll email you a tracking window about four weeks out.
